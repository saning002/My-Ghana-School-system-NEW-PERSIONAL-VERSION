Our Story

A Legacy of Nurturing Young Minds

{% if site and site.history %}{{ site.history }}{% else %} NSMS Little Stars was founded in 2010 with a simple but powerful belief: every child deserves a joyful, nurturing start to their educational journey. What began as a small nursery with just 30 students and 5 teachers has grown into a thriving institution serving over 500 families. {% endif %}

Over the years, we have continually evolved our teaching methods, expanded our facilities, and grown our team to ensure every child who walks through our doors receives the very best in early childhood education.

Today, NSMS Little Stars stands as a pillar of the community — a place where children don't just learn; they flourish.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do — from the classroom to the community.

🌟

Excellence

We strive for the highest standards in every aspect of education and school life, continuously improving our methods and programs.

🤝

💡

Innovation

Community
