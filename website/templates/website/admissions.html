How to Apply

Simple Enrollment in 4 Easy Steps

We've made the process as smooth as possible so you can focus on what matters — your child's future.

Submit Application

Complete and submit our online application form with your child's basic information and preferred program.

Submit Requirements

Bring or upload the required documents. Our admissions team will review your submission promptly.

School Visit

Schedule a tour of our facilities. Meet our teachers and let your child experience our welcoming environment.

Confirm Enrollment

Once accepted, settle the enrollment fee to officially secure your child's place for the school year.

Admission Requirements

Please prepare the following documents when applying.

For the Child

Birth Certificate (PSA)Original or certified true copy
Immunization RecordsUpdated vaccination card or booklet
Recent ID Photo2×2 or passport size (2 copies)
Medical CertificateIssued within the last 6 months
Previous School RecordsReport card (if transferring)

For the Parent / Guardian

Valid Government-issued IDPassport, Driver's License, or PhilSys
Proof of Billing / AddressUtility bill or barangay certificate
Completed Application FormOnline or printed form, fully filled out
Emergency Contact InformationAt least 2 emergency contacts
Enrollment FeeNon-refundable upon acceptance
